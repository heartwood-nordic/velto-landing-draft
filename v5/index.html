Why it matters

Many of the key decisions that shape a new building are made before the information needed to make them well is available. Their full consequences for cost, time, buildability, sustainability and livability only show up later in the process.

By then, changes are difficult and costly, and lead to compromises that weaken both the building and the investment case.

“Many projects never happen. It is the top of the pyramid we see. That is why it is important that the project we choose to take forward is based on as much information as possible.”
Carl Olofsson, BIM lead, JM · translated from Danish

What Velto does

One basis for everyone in the first phase.

Velto lets the developer, the architect and the engineer configure and compare alternatives together, on the same basis, before the investment is committed.

Velto helps you optimise between cost, time, buildability, sustainability and livability, in line with your ambitions.

How it works

Velto is an AI-assisted platform. It helps you draft your building and test it on the plot.

Step 1

Your plot

Velto analyses the site and its surroundings: public transport, schools, shops, demographics, rent levels, recent projects and typical unit sizes nearby, and the relevant parts of the local plan.

Step 2

Your building

Create a simple 3D model on the plot, with facades, roofs and balconies.

Step 3

Your layers

Go into each building part and configure its layers. Velto shows the price difference and the CO₂ impact of every choice.
