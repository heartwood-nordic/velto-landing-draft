The problem we set out to solve

Developers, owners and future residents miss out on large gains, because before anyone commits, it is hard to see what the plot and the building could become, and what each choice is worth. Where it can be done, it is slow, costly and hard to change. Systems are chosen on habit, the plot’s potential is found late, the parties work from different numbers, and buildability is settled after the fact. Every project pays for it, and every new project starts over.

How Velto solves it

With Velto's AI, everyone in the first phase can make the right decisions early and work together to carry them through. Velto knows the site, calculates every choice and remembers what was decided and why.

It does not ask anyone to change how they work. What is decided in Velto carries on into the tools each party already uses.

How Velto fits in

Velto works where the big choices are still open, and hands a locked basis on.

The developer, the architect and the engineer decide on the same basis in Velto. Afterwards, each party keeps working in the tools it already uses.

Tools are named as examples of what the industry uses today, not as integrations.
