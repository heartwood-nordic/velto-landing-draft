Our mission

Buildings could be far better with the right insights and decisions from the very start. That is what Velto is here for.

The problem we set out to solve

Developers, owners and future residents miss out on large gains, because before anyone commits, nobody can see what the plot and the building could become, or what each choice is worth. Systems are chosen on habit, the plot’s potential is found late, the parties work from different numbers, and buildability is settled after the fact. Every project pays for it, and every new project starts over.

How Velto solves it

With Velto's AI, everyone in the first phase can make the right decisions early and work together to carry them through. Velto knows the site, calculates every choice and remembers what was decided and why.

It does not ask anyone to change how they work. What is decided in Velto carries on into the tools each party already uses.

AI-native

Velto’s AI knows the site, runs the numbers and remembers what was decided and why. It was built that way from the first line.
