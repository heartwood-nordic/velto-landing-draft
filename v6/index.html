The problem

The big choices are made before the first real price arrives.

In the brief

The structural system and the main choices are fixed.

At tender

The first real price on those choices arrives.

By then

Changing course means redesigning. So the building gets cut, not improved.

The decisions that shape a new building are made before the information needed to make them well is available. Their consequences for cost, time, buildability, sustainability and livability show up later, when changes are difficult and costly.

The result is compromises that weaken both the building and the investment case.

What Velto does

Velto puts the comparison where it still changes something.
