Why it matters

Good projects get better when the big choices are made with the full picture.

Before you commit, it is hard to see what the plot and the building could become, and what each choice is worth. Where it can be done, it is slow, costly and hard to change.

01

The plot

What the local plan, the area and demand allow is gathered piece by piece, and often late.

02

The building

It is hard to see the building and its options before someone has drawn it.

03

Systems and CO₂

Concrete, timber and hybrid systems are rarely weighed on the project’s own numbers early on. The LCA often arrives when the structure is set, while requirements keep tightening.

04

One basis

The parties work from different numbers, and decisions are reopened at every handover.

What Velto does

Configure and compare together, before the investment is decided.

Velto lets the developer, the architect and the engineer configure and compare alternatives on the same basis. Velto helps you optimise between cost, time, buildability, sustainability and livability, in line with your ambitions.

Sit down with the plot and try different solutions: storeys, layout, systems. What used to take a round of sketches now takes hours, so you find out what you want with the plot and the building before you spend on it.

01

Know what your plot can carry

Velto analyses the plot and its surroundings: public transport, demographics, schools, shops and the relevant parts of the local plan. Rent levels and recent projects nearby are coming.

02

See the building before anyone draws it

A simple 3D model of your project on the plot, with facades, roofs and balconies.

03

See what another system means

Configure the layers of each building part and see the price difference and CO₂ impact of every choice.

04

One basis for partners and investors

An initial report with your decisions and assumptions, the building on its plot, a preliminary LCA and an indicative price comparison.
